Privacy policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

Nimbus runs entirely on your PC. It has no server, and it sends no data to its developer.

What Nimbus can access

When you sign in, Nimbus asks Google for one permission: the drive.file scope. With it, Nimbus can only access the files and folders it created in your Google Drive. It can't see or change anything else in your Drive.

Using that permission, Nimbus:

  • creates a Nimbus folder in your Drive
  • uploads and converts the files you open with it
  • checks that an earlier copy of a file still exists
  • moves old files to your Drive trash, only when you run the cleanup command and confirm it
  • reads your account's email address, to show which account you're signed in with

Where your files go

Your files are uploaded directly from your PC to your own Google Drive. They don't pass through any server run by the developer, and the developer can't access them. Google's privacy policy applies to the data you store in your Google account.

What is stored on your PC

Nimbus keeps the following in %APPDATA%\Nimbus, and never sends it to the developer:

  • your Google sign-in token, which stays only on your PC
  • your settings, your account's email address and the ID of your Nimbus folder
  • a list of the files you've opened (local path, size, modified date and Drive file ID), used to reopen unchanged files without uploading them again

What Nimbus doesn't do

Nimbus has no analytics, telemetry or ads. It doesn't sell or share your data, and it doesn't use your data to train AI models. Its only network connections are to Google, for sign-in and Drive.

Google user data

Nimbus's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

  • To remove Nimbus's access to your account, use myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can also select Sign out in the tray menu, which revokes access and deletes the local token.
  • To delete the data on your PC, delete the %APPDATA%\Nimbus folder.
  • Files that Nimbus uploaded stay in your Drive until you delete them there.

This website

This site has no cookies or analytics, and it's hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' technical data, as described in its privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

Any changes are published on this page, and the date above is updated. Earlier versions are available in the project's history.

Contact

For questions, open an issue on GitHub Issues. Issues are public, so don't include personal information or the contents of your files.
