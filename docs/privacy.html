Privacy policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

Nimbus runs entirely on your PC. It has no server, and it sends no data to its developer.

What Nimbus can access

When you sign in, Nimbus asks Google for one permission: the drive.file scope. With it, Nimbus can only access the files and folders it created in your Google Drive. It can't see or change anything else in your Drive.

Using that permission, Nimbus:

  • creates a Nimbus folder in your Drive
  • uploads and converts the files you open with it
  • checks that an earlier copy of a file still exists, and whether it changed in Google
  • downloads your edits from Google to the file on your PC, when you select Save to computer or when Save back automatically is on (it's on by default and checks documents you opened in the last 24 hours)
  • changes a file's sharing, only when you ask it to (see Sharing links)
  • moves old files to your Drive trash, only when you run the cleanup command and confirm it
  • reads your account's email address, to show which account you're signed in with

Where your files go

Your files are uploaded directly from your PC to your own Google Drive. They don't pass through any server run by the developer, and the developer can't access them. Google's privacy policy applies to the data you store in your Google account.

Sharing links

Files that Nimbus uploads are private to your account unless you share them. In the tray menu, Copy link offers two options:

  • Only for me copies the file's link and doesn't change who can access it.
  • Anyone with the link can view… asks you to confirm, then gives anyone who has the link permission to view the file. They don't need to sign in, and they can pass the link on. Nimbus can't see or control who opens it.

To end public access, select Stop sharing in the tray menu, or change the file's sharing in Google Drive.

What is stored on your PC

Nimbus keeps the following in %APPDATA%\Nimbus, and never sends it to the developer:

  • your Google sign-in token, which stays only on your PC
  • your settings, your account's email address and the ID of your Nimbus folder
  • a list of the files you've opened (local path, size, modified date, when you last opened it and Drive file ID), used for the recent files menu, to save edits back and to reopen unchanged files without uploading them again
  • backups of your files, described below

Backups

Before Nimbus replaces a file on your PC with your edits from Google, it saves a copy of that file in %APPDATA%\Nimbus\backups. These are complete copies, so they contain whatever the file contained. They stay on your PC and aren't uploaded anywhere.

Nimbus keeps up to five backups of each file and deletes the oldest ones. Automatic saves make at most one backup an hour. Backups stay on your PC after you sign out or delete the original file, until you delete them.

Update checks

Nimbus checks GitHub for new versions up to once every 24 hours, by requesting the latest release of this project from api.github.com. If you install an update, Nimbus downloads it from GitHub too. Updates are checked against a signature before they're installed.

These requests don't include your Google account, your files or your settings. Like any web request, they show GitHub your IP address, and they identify the app as Nimbus and its version number. GitHub's privacy statement applies to them. The developer can see how many times each release was downloaded, but not who downloaded it.

To stop these requests, open Settings… in the tray menu and set Updates to Off.

What Nimbus doesn't do

Nimbus has no analytics, telemetry or ads. It doesn't sell or share your data, and it doesn't use your data to train AI models. Its only network connections are to Google, for sign-in and Drive, and to GitHub, for updates.

Google user data

Nimbus's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

  • To remove Nimbus's access to your account, use myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can also select Sign out in the tray menu, which revokes access and deletes the local token.
  • To delete the data on your PC, including backups, delete the %APPDATA%\Nimbus folder.
  • Files that Nimbus uploaded stay in your Drive until you delete them there.

This website

This site has no cookies or analytics, and it's hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' technical data, as described in its privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

Any changes are published on this page, and the date above is updated. Earlier versions are available in the project's history.

Contact

For questions, open an issue on GitHub Issues. Issues are public, so don't include personal information or the contents of your files.
